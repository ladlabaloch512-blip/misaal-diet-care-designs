Programs in Detail

Every Program, Explained

What each program actually involves, and who it is made for. Your exact plan is always written after your own assessment.

Personalised Diet Plans

A complete lifestyle and meal guide written only for you: your foods, your timings, your routine. It is built after the four-step assessment, fits your kitchen, your culture and your budget, and it changes as you change.

Best for: anyone starting out, or anyone whose generic diet charts have already failed.

Fat Loss

Steady, sustainable fat reduction that protects your muscle and metabolism. No crash diets and no banned food groups - realistic portions, weekly progress reviews, and adjustments until the downward trend holds.

Best for: clients who want lasting weight loss without starving, and without the rebound.

Weight Gain

Nutrient-dense, calorie-smart plans for healthy mass and muscle. Our client Ali Nisar started at 46 kg and gained 1.5 kg in his first week on a guided plan, with his progress tracked every single week.

Best for: underweight clients, and anyone who eats more yet still cannot gain.

PCOS Care

The core speciality of Dietitian Areesha Misaal. Plans target hormonal balance and insulin resistance with practical, anti-inflammatory eating, paired with lifestyle management and close monitoring.

Best for: women managing PCOS symptoms, irregular cycles and stubborn weight.

Clinical Nutrition

Medical nutrition therapy for illness, recovery and long-term disease care. Your lab results guide the plan, and nutrition is coordinated with your medical treatment - never against it.

Best for: patients whose condition needs diet to be managed as part of treatment.

Sports Nutrition

Fuel for training: strength, stamina and faster recovery. Meals and portions are timed around your workouts and competition days - a subject MIND also teaches in its sports nutrition workshops.

Best for: athletes, gym-goers and active clients who train regularly.

The Method

How We Work With You

The same four steps sit behind every MDC plan, from a first fat loss chart to full clinical nutrition.

Assess - We Listen First

Before anything is written, we study your health history, dietary habits, lab results, lifestyle, culture and budget. A plan only works if it fits the life you actually live.

Estimate - Your Numbers, Set Properly

Your energy and protein needs are calculated from your age, activity and clinical status - not copied from a generic chart made for somebody else.

Build - Familiar Food, Real Portions

The plan is built from foods you already know and like, in portions and timings that stay practical on a normal, busy day.

Monitor - Reviewed With You

Progress and tolerance are reviewed together, and the plan is adjusted as you change. You are never handed a chart and left alone with it.
